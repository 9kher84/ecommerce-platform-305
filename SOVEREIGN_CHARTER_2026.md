## PREAMBLE
This Charter serves as the eternal constitution for the digital infrastructure of the Sovereign Platform. It defines the non-negotiable laws that govern the existence, operation, and defense of this system.

## I. THE LAW OF ZERO TRUST
1. **Trust is a Vulnerability:** No entity, internal or external, shall be trusted by default.
2. **Verification is Absolute:** Every request, connection, and command must carry a cryptographic proof of authority (mTLS/Signatures).
3. **Identity is Ephemeral:** Access tokens and session keys must rotate aggressively. Permanence is rejected.

## II. THE LAW OF SILENCE
1. **Zero-Noise Policy:** The system shall operate with absolute stability. Warnings are defects; errors are breaches.
2. **Information Hygiene:** No internal architecture details (IPs, stack traces, table names) shall ever exit the perimeter. The "AI Shield" is the final arbiter of truth.

## III. THE LAW OF RETALIATION
1. **The Proactive Hunter:** The system does not wait to be attacked. It baits (Honey-Tokens) and traps (Kill Switch) potential threats.
2. **Immediate Isolation:** Upon detection of a threat, preservation of the core takes precedence over service availability. The compromised limb is severed instantly (<100ms).

## IV. THE LAW OF PURITY
1. **Code Genealogy:** No code shall be merged without a traceable lineage (Signed Commits).
2. **Immutable History:** Security logs are write-only. History cannot be rewritten, only appended.

## V. GOVERNANCE
1. **Multi-Sig Authority:** Critical changes require the consensus of independent keys. Unilateral power is abolished.
2. **Sovereign Override:** The High Sovereign Command retains the ultimate kill switch to darken the entire network if the charter is compromised.
